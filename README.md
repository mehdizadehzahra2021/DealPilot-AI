DealPilot AI — B2B Lead Qualification & Outreach Blueprint

From Market Research to Qualified Sales Opportunities

DealPilot AI is an interactive HTML product blueprint for a proposed AI-assisted B2B prospecting and sales outreach platform. It demonstrates how company research, evidence-based lead qualification, transparent scoring, and personalized outreach preparation could be organized into a single workflow.

Project status: Interactive frontend prototype and product workflow documentation. This repository does not contain a production backend or live integrations.

Problem Statement

B2B sales teams often spend significant time researching potential customers, evaluating whether their businesses fit an ideal customer profile (ICP), and writing personalized outreach messages.

DealPilot AI explores a workflow designed to make this process more structured, transparent, and reviewable.

Proposed Solution

The blueprint describes a workflow that combines:

Ideal Customer Profile (ICP): Define target industries, company characteristics, business needs, and exclusion criteria.
Company Discovery: Identify and organize potential prospects from permitted sources.
Evidence-Based Research: Associate findings with supporting sources and distinguish verified information from assumptions.
Lead Qualification: Evaluate prospects against five weighted criteria and prioritize them using configurable scoring.
Transparent Reasoning: Label evidence as verified, inference, or unknown rather than presenting assumptions as facts.
Personalized Outreach: Prepare draft messages grounded in the selected prospect’s available evidence.
Human Review: Require approval before any proposed outreach is sent.
Interactive Prototype Features

The HTML prototype includes an interactive product explanation, navigation between sections, configurable scoring concepts, example prospect records, and a workflow diagram with selectable steps and alternative decision paths.

The interface is intended to communicate the proposed product logic and MVP scope.

Demo Data and Transparency

The prototype uses fictional example companies for demonstration. These records are not verified real prospects.

Evidence labels and scoring are intended to make the distinction between documented facts, inferences, and unknown information visible to the user.

Current Limitations

The current version is a product prototype and workflow blueprint, not a fully operational AI application.

It does not currently implement live company research, production lead-scoring services, backend persistence, external search integrations, or actual message delivery. These would require additional implementation and appropriate integrations.

Responsible Use

Any future implementation should respect data-source terms, privacy requirements, and applicable outreach regulations. Inferences should remain distinguishable from verified facts, and human approval should precede outbound communication.
