DealPilot AI

From Market Research to Qualified Sales Opportunities.

Product explanation and workflow: why it is needed and how it works.

This document describes a proposed product. No integration, company research or customer result described here exists yet.

AExecutive Summary

DealPilot AI helps a business find potential B2B customers, read their public information, collect evidence of possible business needs, judge fit against the user's offering, and prepare personalized outreach that a person reviews before anything is sent.

Who it is for

Small teams that sell B2B services or software and prospect by hand: automation agencies, software firms, consultancies and SaaS vendors.

The problem

Prospecting is slow and inconsistent. People guess whether a company needs a solution, then write every email from scratch.

How it differs from a lead-list tool

A list tool returns names and contacts. DealPilot AI keeps the evidence behind every judgment, scores transparently, and writes only what that evidence supports.

Value proposition

Fewer hours on research, consistent qualification criteria, and outreach that can be traced to sources, with a human decision before sending.

Why combine research, evidence, AI reasoning and workflow automation?

  • Web research supplies facts. Without it, AI invents plausible text.
  • Evidence-based qualification separates what is known from what is guessed.
  • AI reasoning reads unstructured pages and drafts language, which rules alone cannot do well.
  • Workflow automation makes the process repeatable, retryable and auditable.
Honest limits. DealPilot AI does not discover hidden needs with certainty and cannot guarantee sales. It surfaces possible opportunities. Every finding is labelled Verified Inference or Unknown.
Presentation summary. DealPilot AI turns an ideal-customer description into a ranked, evidence-backed list of companies and reviewable email drafts, using n8n for orchestration, Python for deterministic logic, Ollama for local language tasks and PostgreSQL for traceable storage. Nothing is sent without human approval.

BThe Business Problem

Traditional prospecting is a chain of manual steps, each of which loses time or consistency.

Traditional workflow

  1. Search for companies by hand.
  2. Open each website.
  3. Read services and business information.
  4. Guess whether the company needs the solution.
  5. Search for decision-makers.
  6. Write each email individually.
  7. Track follow-ups in notes or spreadsheets.

With DealPilot AI (proposed)

  1. Define the customer profile once.
  2. Discover and de-duplicate candidates.
  3. Collect public information with stored sources.
  4. Label evidence as verified, inferred or unknown.
  5. Score with visible reasons and a confidence level.
  6. Draft emails grounded in that evidence.
  7. Human approves, edits or rejects; outcomes are recorded.

Costs of the manual process

  • Time: research effort grows linearly with every company.
  • Inconsistency: two people judge the same company differently, and criteria are rarely written down.
  • Missed opportunities: good-fit companies are skipped because research is tiring; weak fits get contacted because nothing was checked.
  • No learning loop: without recorded evidence and outcomes, nobody can tell which criteria work.
